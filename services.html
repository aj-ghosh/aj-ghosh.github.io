Services

Practice Operations Setup & Support

For established insurance and advisory practices. I help you organize prospect follow-up, new-business workflows, and team responsibilities inside the systems you already use — then provide ongoing reviews and adjustments to keep the practice organized.

Scope

The three workflows

Configured for your practice type — not generic templates dropped in and abandoned.

01

Prospect follow-up

Opportunity stages, next actions, deadlines, ownership, and follow-up rules. You see at a glance which opportunities need attention this week — and exactly who owns each one. Nothing ages silently.

02

New business & onboarding

Required steps, outstanding items, handoffs, and completion status. For insurance practices: application requirements and underwriting status tracking. For RIAs: agreements, account opening, transfers, and onboarding tasks — the same way every time.

03

Existing-client service

Review schedules, service requests, task assignments, and reminders. Full visibility into what's due, who's responsible, and what your clients are waiting on — before they have to ask.

Pricing

Software licenses stay yours — you own the system, the documentation, and the data in it.

Phase B · Ongoing

Monthly Operations Support

$750/mo

pilot pricing · three-month initial term, beginning after implementation

  • Up to four total service hours each month
  • Preparation of your operations scorecard: overdue follow-ups, opportunities without next actions, aging onboarding items, upcoming service obligations
  • One 45–60 minute review with you and your assistant — priorities, exceptions, assignments
  • Agreed workflow updates or training

How the four hours work: scorecard preparation, the review meeting itself, and any updates all count toward the four hours. Not included: daily data entry, prospect calling, appointment setting, assistant recruiting, major new implementations. Your team stays responsible for its daily tasks — I keep the system they work in honest.

Fit

Who this is for — and who it isn't

Good fit

  • Established practice with real prospect and client volume
  • Follow-up and coordination are inconsistent — you feel the friction
  • An assistant or team member who will operate the system day to day
  • An owner willing to enforce the process once it's built

Not a fit

  • You need new leads generated — this organizes the business you have; it doesn't create prospects
  • No one on the team will maintain records or complete tasks
  • You're looking for someone to run the practice for you day to day
