About

I work behind the scenes of financial practices.

The short version

I'm AJ Ghosh — a licensed insurance professional affiliated with the New York Life ecosystem, with background at New York Life, Northwestern Mutual, and Eagle Strategies LLC. For the past decade, my work has lived behind the scenes: pipelines, practice workflows, prospect follow-up systems, and the operational plumbing that lets producers spend their time producing.

I haven't spent that decade sitting across from retail clients. That's deliberate, and it's the point: I know how financial practices actually run from the inside — where opportunities stall, where onboarding breaks down, and where a good assistant with a good system outperforms a great producer with a bad one.

This consulting practice exists for one reason: to give established agents and advisors the operational backbone their production deserves. I build the system, train your team to run it, and keep it honest with a monthly review.

How I work

Structure before strategy. Before chasing new growth, get a clear view of what's already in motion — every opportunity with a status, an owner, and a next action with a date.

Your systems, not mine. I implement inside the CRM and tools you already pay for. No new subscriptions, no rip-and-replace.

Built to outlast me. Everything is documented and your team is trained. You should be able to run the system without me — you keep me because the monthly review keeps finding things worth fixing.
