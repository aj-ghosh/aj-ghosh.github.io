The problem

The practice is growing. The operation isn't keeping up.

Most established practices don't have a revenue problem. They have a follow-through problem — and it compounds quietly.

01

Prospects go quiet

Opportunities sit in the CRM with no next action and no owner. Follow-up depends on memory, and memory loses to a busy week. Every stalled prospect is revenue you already earned the right to close.

02

Onboarding improvises itself

Applications, paperwork, transfers, and handoffs get coordinated ad hoc — differently every time, by whoever happens to be free. Clients feel the disorganization before you do.

03

The team runs on verbal

Responsibilities live in people's heads. When someone's out, overloaded, or leaves, the process leaves with them. You're the backstop for everything, which is exactly why you can't step back.

What I build

Three workflows. One operating rule.

I implement the processes your practice needs, train your team to use them, and help keep them working. Every opportunity and task ends up with a defined status, an owner, and a next action with a date.

01

Prospect follow-up

Opportunity stages, next actions, deadlines, ownership, and follow-up rules — a clear view of which opportunities need attention, and who's responsible for each.

02

New business & onboarding

Required steps, outstanding items, handoffs, and completion status. For insurance practices: application and underwriting tracking. For RIAs: agreements, account opening, and transfers.

03

Existing-client service

Review schedules, service requests, task assignments, and reminders — visibility into what's due, who's doing it, and what your clients are waiting on.
